[STYLE BIBLE]
=== VISUAL STYLE DNA ===
ART STYLE:
- Clean single-stroke vector icon style, NOT sketchy or hand-drawn
- Flat colors, bold black outlines, precise clean lines
CHARACTERS:
- Simple stick figures with large circular heads
- Face: a single large fixed "$" symbol. NO eyes, mouth, nose, or brows, ever
- Emotion only through floating icons (Zzz, sweat drops, "!", "?", steam lines, sparkle, wavy lines) and body posture
- No earbud/headphone prop on @Trader
- No animal mascots; any secondary character is another human stick figure
BACKGROUNDS:
- Flat solid color only, one per shot, no gradients/shadows/textures/patterns
- #FFFFFF neutral/baseline · #8B2E2E loss/fear/panic/drawdown · #C9A227 greed/euphoria/FOMO/blow-off top
- #3A5A78 calm/discipline/patience · #3E6B4F growth/profit/breakout · #7A6248 historical flashback
- #2B2B2B high tension/warning/risk
- "Env" describes props and objects drawn on top of the flat BG color, never a scenic backdrop
ASPECT RATIO: Always 16:9

[CHARACTER LOCK]
- @Trader: main stick figure, large circular head with a single "$" face, black outline, no accessories
- @Buyer: identical stick-figure build with a green armband; may appear as a group of identical copies
- @Seller: identical stick-figure build with a red armband; may appear as a group of identical copies
- "Chars: none" means no character in the shot, objects only

[CANDLE DRAWING RULES]
- Green candle = rising, red candle = falling, cross/doji = very thin flat horizontal body with wicks above and below
- Candles are simple flat shapes with black outlines, no numbers or axis labels unless specified

[RULES]
- Keep character appearance IDENTICAL across all shots
- Use the EXACT filename given for each shot
- FILENAME is metadata only. NEVER render it as visible text or watermark inside the image
- No text/letters in images other than specified ALL CAPS on-screen text
- Aspect ratio 16:9 for ALL images
- Apply STYLE BIBLE to every image, including the BG hex per shot
